Who does what

Your team

  1. Account holder enables Kite Connect at developers.kite.trade and keeps the API key and secret private.
  2. Create a Telegram group, then a bot through @BotFather. Keep the bot token private.
  3. Sign up for Oracle Cloud free tier, Mumbai region.
  4. Pick a project folder on your PC for the code.
  5. Write down your risk limits in rupees: per trade, per day.
  6. Build a starting watchlist of 15–30 stocks.
  7. Paste every key into the server's config file yourselves. Never send keys in chat.

Claude

  1. Write the blueprint: data formats, folders, config file.

  3. Write step-by-step guides for Kite Connect, Telegram and Oracle Cloud.

  5. Run backtests of the strategy families and report results.

Zerodha: how to get live data

Trading stays on your Zerodha account either way. The open choice is where the bot gets its live prices.

OptionHow it worksCostTrade-off
A · Kite Connect data plan suggestedBot reads live prices from your Zerodha accountMonthly fee (check current price on developers.kite.trade)One account, best docs, includes history for backtests, and Streak later is native
B · Free Dhan account for dataKeep trading on Zerodha, open a Dhan account only for its free data API₹0A second account to open and keep active

Kite Connect needs a fresh login token every day. The bot will send a login link to Telegram each morning, and the account holder taps it.

Ground rules

  • No agent places orders until phase 4, and then only inside hard limits your team sets.
  • The account belongs to one person. Don't pool teammates' money into it; that needs SEBI registration. Each member can follow the alerts on their own account.
  • Alerts and briefs are research. Every trade decision stays with a person.
  • Keys and passwords never go in chat.
